# last-mile M1 Phase 4 (Talk) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use `- [ ]`. Seat-driven in this repo: ZCode and MiniMax Code implement, and Claude reviews and commits.

**Goal:** **Squads coordinate by talking and you hear them** (spec §10, phase 4).

- an utterance is a **score**, not a string (D12): words timed against pitch, energy, rate, emphasis and pause lanes, plus act, intent, affect, addressee and subject tags;
- `lm-core` expands the score into lanes **deterministically** and **degrades** it per listener (distance, walls, radio) — an agent hears a *worse* copy, not the truth;
- the decision model picks **what** to say (speech act, addressee, intent, affect); the **wording** comes from a template phrasebook or a small local LLM; **silence is a legal answer** (D31);
- the human talks too: push-to-talk → ASR → an utterance with real prosody; the half-duplex gate makes the agents yield (D28); the tutor ladder teaches the verbs (D29);
- and the round still replays **bit-exact**: every utterance is a logged decision.

**Architecture:**
- `lm-core` gains the score, the notation, the hearing physics and the talk metrics (all fixed point, no floats, no networking):
  - `utterance.rs`: the score types (`Utterance`, `Word`, `Channel`, `SpeechAct`, `Intent`, `Affect`, `Addressee`, `Subject`, `Prosody`);
  - `opal.rs`: the OPAL-style notation — a parser that compiles text into a score, and a renderer back to text (fleet/07);
  - `hearing.rs`: per-channel range, wall attenuation, lane degradation, the audibility set;
  - `talk.rs`: `TalkParams` (the round's talk rules) and `TalkMetrics` (phase 5's metrics);
- `lm-core` also gains **`Kind::Utterance`** and **`PROTO_VERSION 2`**, behind a **version gate** that keeps every phase-1 golden hash valid and replays phase-1 logs unchanged;
- `lm-agents` (the Thor) gains the **utterance gates** and the **realizer** (template phrasebook by default, LFM2-1.2B optional);
- a new crate `lm-voice` holds the **TTS/ASR traits**, the stub, the Riva placeholder, the bark cache and the prosody port;
- `lm-host-model` / `lm-host` (the ProArt) gain the **half-duplex gate**, the **microphone capture**, the map's speech ticks and the HUD's talk counters.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 1.99; `serde` + `postcard` (the score is a decision, so it rides the wire and the log); `ureq` 2.x (blocking HTTP with keep-alive, as phase 3) for Riva and the prosody service; `toml` for the phrasebook. Phase 1–3 crates.

**Spec:** spec §3 (decision kinds, fixed point), §4 (visibility and hearing are numbers), §5 (agents, utterances), §6 (ProArt client, human, audio), §9 (testing), §10 phase 4, §11 (parameters). Decisions D7, D12, D13, D19, D25, D28, D29, D31, D37, D49, D53, D55–D58. Evidence: `docs/research/fleet/02-lessons.md`, `docs/research/fleet/07-plato-music.md`, `docs/research/fleet/08-communicator-and-tev1.md`, `docs/research/forclaude2/03-voice-and-comms.md`, `docs/research/forclaude2/09-utterance-score.md`, and the `communicator` repo (`SuperInstance/communicator`).

## Global Constraints

- **`lm-core` stays model-free and float-free.** No model may be required to replay a round: every utterance enters the sim only as a logged `Decision` with its `Provenance`. `scripts/check-no-float.sh` keeps `f32`/`f64` out of `crates/lm-core/src` — the lanes, the clarity and the metrics are all integers.
- **The score is the truth; the heard copy is the truth as degraded.** The log carries the full score and replays exactly; the *degradation* is recomputed by the sim from the world, so it is reproducible too. Nothing is inferred from audio.
- **The wording is separate from the choice.** The decision model chooses the act, the addressee, the intent, the affect and the subject; only the *wording* comes from the phrasebook or the LLM (D37, `utter.realizer`). An LLM may never choose a target, and a malformed line is a **refusal** (D26), never a guess.
- **Silence is an outcome.** A gate whose presence gate (D53) says "this is not even here" produces no utterance at all. Silence is not an error and never costs airtime.
- **Never average models, never fabricate a default.** Every backend has its own threshold (phase 3's `min_confidence`); a refusal falls back to the template or to silence, and the fallback is logged.
- **The audio is for the human.** TTS is requested only for lines the human's listener position can hear. The sim never depends on whether the human heard anything.
- **Every tunable is a parameter** (D19): the round's talk rules travel in the header (`TalkParams`), the client rules in the client config (`audio.*`, `human.*`, `utter.*`). Defaults are listed in the tables below.
- **The talk params are not branchable.** They change what the round *is* (who hears what), so they join the header hash and stay out of `Params::BRANCH_FIELDS`.
- Commits as in Phase 1 (small, conventional, every commit message ends with the `Co-Authored-By` line).

> **Note on a spec conflict:** spec §6 says human prosody capture is M2; D53 (2026-10-28, later) places *phase-4 human prosody capture* in this phase and says to reuse `communicator` rather than rebuild it. This plan follows **D53**. The spec line is corrected in the same commit that lands Task 6.

## Review Focus

1. **The proto bump silently breaks the old logs and the goldens.** `Kind::Utterance` must be **appended** (the existing variant indices 0–5 keep their bytes), the header's `talk` field must be **appended**, and the version gate must **re-root** a phase-1 log's chain without touching a single state hash → `kind_utterance_is_appended_not_inserted`, `phase1_goldens_unchanged`, `v1_log_replays_under_v2` (Task 1).
2. **The hearing physics makes everybody deaf** (a range in the wrong unit, a wall counted twice, the radio not reaching the squad). Then the squads stop coordinating and phase 4 plays exactly like phase 1, silently → `voice_reaches_a_squad_mate_within_24_metres`, `a_wall_halves_the_clarity`, `radio_reaches_the_squad_through_walls` (Task 3) and `an_order_is_heard_and_taken_up` (Task 9).
3. **The human's turn ends on a one-second pause.** Casey's pauses have a median of 0.72 s and **33.8 % are ≥ 1 s**: a 1 s cut-off truncates a third of the human's commands → `one_second_pause_does_not_end_the_turn`, `three_seconds_of_silence_ends_the_turn`, `push_to_talk_ends_the_turn_on_release` (Task 6).
4. **The agents talk over the human**, or the gate *queues* the dropped frames and fires them 400 ms later (D28 says dropped, not queued) → `held_frames_are_dropped_not_queued`, `agent_audio_resumes_after_the_400ms_hang` (Task 7).
5. **The LLM realizer returns garbage, or the Thor is down.** A malformed line must not reach the log: it is a refusal, and the agent falls back to the template or to silence → `bad_realizer_output_is_a_refusal`, `llm_down_falls_back_to_the_template`, `a_refused_line_says_nothing` (Task 4).

### Task 1: The Utterance decision kind, and the version gate

**Interfaces:**
- `lm-core/src/utterance.rs` (new), all fixed point:

  | type | variants | notes |
  |---|---|---|
  | `Channel` | `Shout`, `Voice`, `Whisper`, `Radio` | the four channels of spec §4; `Radio` reaches the squad only |
  | `SpeechAct` | `Command`, `Question`, `Statement`, `Callout` | the **gesture** (communicator's GESTURE bank, 4 classes) |
  | `Intent` | `Order`, `Ack`, `Warn`, `Query`, `Report` | spec §3's intent tags; `Order` is the one the metrics count |
  | `Affect` | `Calm`, `Urgent`, `Uncertain` | the **affect bucket** — the bark cache's third key |
  | `Addressee` | `Squad`, `All`, `Agent(u16)` | who the line is for |
  | `Subject` | `Beacon(u8)`, `Enemy(u16)`, `Direction(u8)`, `None` | **what the line is about** — the game-relevant content |
  | `Prosody` | `Synthesized`, `Measured`, `Absent` | where the lanes came from; `Absent` is *not* zero (communicator's rule 4) |
  | `Word` | `{ text: String, t_on: Tick, t_off: Tick, pitch_steps: i8, energy_q16: u32, rate_q16: u32, emphasis_q16: u32, pause_after_ticks: u16 }` | one lyric event per word |
  | `Utterance` | `{ speaker: u16, channel, addressee, act, intent, affect, subject, prosody, words: Vec<Word> }` | the score |

  - Fixed point: `pitch_steps` is semitones from the speaker's baseline, clamped to `-24..=24`; `energy_q16`/`rate_q16`/`emphasis_q16` are Q16 (`65_536` = 1.0, rate may reach `131_072` = 2.0).
  - `Utterance::duration_ticks(&self) -> u32` = the last word's `t_off` − the first word's `t_on` (the trailing rest is silence and does not count).
  - `Utterance::prose(&self) -> String` = the words joined by one space (the TTS text and the bark key's raw form).
  - Every type gets a `StateHash` impl with an explicit `tag()` (declaration order), like `Source`.
- `Kind::Utterance(Utterance)` in `crates/lm-core/src/decision.rs`, **appended after `Kind::Marker`** (tag `6`; the existing tags `0..=5` keep their bytes, so phase-1 decisions still decode).
- `Header` gains `pub talk: TalkParams`, **appended after `lineage`**, hashed after it; `PROTO_VERSION: u16 = 2`.
- `TalkParams` (in `lm-core/src/talk.rs`) — the round's talk rules:

  | field | default | meaning |
  |---|---|---|
  | `range_shout_units` | `61_440` (60 m) | shout range |
  | `range_voice_units` | `25_600` (25 m) | voice range |
  | `range_whisper_units` | `5_120` (5 m) | whisper range |
  | `radio_is_squad` | `true` | radio reaches the whole squad, no range |
  | `wall_loss_q16` | `32_768` (0.5) | clarity multiplier per wall crossed |
  | `min_clarity_q16` | `1_536` (≈0.023) | below this, nothing is heard |
  | `clarity_pitch_q16` | `49_152` (0.75) | above this the pitch survives intact |
  | `clarity_rate_q16` | `16_384` (0.25) | above this the pace and the pauses survive |
  | `act_clarity_q16` | `32_768` (0.5) | above this the speech act is understood |

  | `airtime_s` | `20` | per-agent airtime budget per round |
  | `airtime_on_exhaust` | `"reflex"` | `"reflex"` \| `"freeze"` |
  | `order_uptake_window_ticks` | `150` (5 s) | how long an order stays live |
  | `enemy_overhears_voice` | `true` | enemies hear voice and shout in range |

### Task 2: The OPAL-style utterance notation

**Interfaces:**
- `lm-core/src/opal.rs` (new):
  - `pub struct TalkContext { pub speaker: u16, pub roster: &[RosterEntry] }` — who is speaking and who exists (used to resolve a squad colour).
  - `pub fn parse(ctx: &TalkContext, text: &str) -> Result<Utterance, OpalError>` — the **compiler**: text → score.
  - `pub fn render(u: &Utterance) -> String` — the score → text.
  - `pub enum OpalError { Empty, BadHeader, UnknownKey, BadAccent, BadPitch, BadLength, ForeignSquad, NoSubject }` — a refusal with a name, never a guess.
- **The grammar** (the fleet/07 sketch, made final):
  - a header line: `@<scope> <act> [key=value …]`, where the scope is `squad` | `all` | `agent<N>`, the act is `say` | `ask` | `order` | `call` (→ `Statement` | `Question` | `Command` | `Callout`), and the keys — **in any order** — are `to=` (`squad` | `all` | `agent<N>` | a squad colour), `intent=` (`order` | `ack` | `warn` | `query` | `report`), `affect=` (`calm` | `urgent` | `uncertain`), `subject=` (`beacon<N>` | `enemy<N>` | `dir<N>` | `none`), `channel=` (`shout` | `voice` | `whisper` | `radio`);
  - a word line: `<word> p<±n> a<.|!|!!> l<n> [~rest<n>]`.
- **The compiler's laws** (all integer):
  - `pitch_steps` = the `p` value, clamped to ±24; the accent maps `.` → `0`, `!` → `32_768`, `!!` → `65_536`;
  - `word_ticks = beat_ticks × l`, at least `min_word_ticks`; `t_on` accumulates from the previous word's `t_off`, `t_off = t_on + word_ticks`;
  - `pause_after_ticks = beat_ticks × rest` (0 when there is no `~rest`);
  - `energy_q16 = min(65_536, affect_energy_q16 + emphasis_q16 / 2)` with `Calm` → `16_384`, `Uncertain` → `24_576`, `Urgent` → `49_152`;
  - `rate_q16 = min(131_072, (65_536 / l) × affect_rate_q16 / 65_536)` with `Calm` → `58_704` (0.9×), `Uncertain` → `65_536` (1.0×), `Urgent` → `78_432` (1.2×);
  - the defaults: `channel = Voice`, `subject = None`, `prosody = Synthesized`, `act` from the act word.
- **The renderer** writes the canonical form: `@<scope> <act> to=… intent=… affect=… [subject=…] [channel=…]`, then one word line per word, two-space indented, single spaces inside: `<word> p<n> a<a> l<n> [~rest<n>]`. `p0` (not `p+0`).
- The **fleet/07 sketch** parses and renders back to exactly:
  ```
  @squad say to=squad intent=order affect=urgent
    hold p+2 a! l2
    the p0 a. l1
    north p+3 a!! l3
    beacon p+1 a. l2 ~rest1
  ```
- [ ] Tests:
  - `opal_parses_the_fleet_sketch` — the sketch compiles to the expected words: `hold` (`p+2`, `!`, 10 ticks), `the` (`p0`, `.`, 5), `north` (`p+3`, `!!`, 15), `beacon` (`p+1`, `.`, 10, rest 5) — the last word ends on tick 40, the trailing rest runs to tick 45, and the utterance's `duration_ticks` is **40** (the rest is silence);
  - `opal_renders_canonically` — `render(parse(sketch))` equals the canonical text above, byte for byte (the sketch's `to=red` is resolved at compile time against the roster and rendered back as the neutral `to=squad`);
  - `opal_round_trips_to_the_same_score` — `parse(ctx, render(u)) == u` for every score in a table of ten (each act, each affect, each channel);
  - `opal_is_deterministic` — the same text compiles to the same bytes a thousand times;
  - `opal_rejects_a_bad_header` — `OpalError::BadHeader` for `@squad maybe intent=order affect=calm`; `UnknownKey` for `mood=happy`; `BadAccent` for `a?`; `BadPitch` for `p99`;
  - `opal_refuses_a_foreign_squad` — `to=blue` from a red speaker is `ForeignSquad`, never silently `Squad`;
  - `opal_refuses_an_empty_line` — `OpalError::Empty`, and an empty utterance is never compiled (a refusal, not a zero-length line).

### Task 3: Hearing physics in `lm-core`

**Interfaces:**
- `lm-core/src/hearing.rs` (new):
  - `pub fn clarity(channel: Channel, dist_units: u32, walls: u8, p: &TalkParams) -> u32` — the law, in Q16:
    - radio → `65_536` (no distance, no walls);
    - otherwise `65_536 − (dist_units << 16) / range_units`, then `× wall_loss_q16 >> 16` for **each** wall crossed;
    - `0` when the distance is beyond the range.
  - `pub fn walls_between(grid: &Grid, a: CellXY, b: CellXY) -> u8` (in `world/los.rs`, reusing the existing `line_cells`): the number of **`Wall`** cells on the straight line between the two cells (cover does not attenuate; the endpoints are exempt).
  - `pub fn degrade(u: &Utterance, clarity_q16: u32, listener: u16, p: &TalkParams) -> Heard` — the listener's copy:
    - `energy_q16' = energy_q16 × clarity >> 16`; a word whose degraded energy is below `word_drop_q16` is **dropped**;
    - `pitch_steps'` = the pitch, flattened toward 0 by `clarity / clarity_pitch_q16` when the clarity is below `clarity_pitch_q16`; on radio, clamped to ±`radio_pitch_steps`;
    - `rate_q16'` = the rate above `clarity_rate_q16`, otherwise `65_536` (the pace is lost); `pause_after_ticks'` = 0 below that threshold;
    - `act` and `intent` are `Some` only above `act_clarity_q16` / `intent_clarity_q16` — below them the listener **knows the words but not the point**;
    - radio drops a word deterministically when `fnv1a(utterance_id, word_index, listener) % 65_536 < radio_drop_q16` — a hash, never an RNG, so it replays.
  - `pub fn audible_to(u: &Utterance, speaker: Pos, listener: Pos, listener_squad: u8, speaker_squad: u8, grid: &Grid, p: &TalkParams) -> bool` — the audibility query the host asks before requesting TTS.
- `Heard` (in `utterance.rs`): `{ id: u64, speaker: u16, channel: Channel, act: Option<SpeechAct>, intent: Option<Intent>, affect: Affect, subject: Subject, clarity_q16: u32, words: Vec<Word>, spoken_words: u16, started_tick: Tick, ended_tick: Tick, ongoing: bool }`.
- `ScoreView` gains `pub heard: Vec<Heard>` — every utterance this agent has heard **since the last view update**, ongoing ones included (the pre-draft needs an utterance that has only *started*).
- `SquadKnowledge` gains `pub told: BTreeMap<u16, (CellXY, Tick)>` — facts the squad was **told** (spec §4: "you only know what your squad can see or has been told"), keyed by the enemy id, exactly like `last_seen`.
- The **sim** applies an utterance decision on its `apply_tick`: it computes the listener set (the addressee's squad for `Squad`/`Agent`, every agent in earshot for `All`, plus **enemies** for voice and shout when `enemy_overhears_voice`), the clarity per listener, and the degraded copy per listener; it adds the utterance to the addressed squad's knowledge and records a `TalkRecord`.
- [ ] Tests:
  - `voice_reaches_a_squad_mate_within_24_metres` — at 24.0 m the clarity is `2_622` (above `min_clarity_q16 1_536`) and the words arrive; at 24.5 m the clarity is `1_311` and the listener hears **nothing** (the audible edge of a 25 m channel is ≈24.4 m, not 25 m);
  - `a_wall_halves_the_clarity` — the same pair, with and without a wall: the clarity with a wall is exactly half (rounded down), two walls are a quarter;
  - `radio_reaches_the_squad_through_walls` — the radio's clarity is `65_536` with ten walls between the pair;
  - `shout_outranges_voice_outranges_whisper` — at 30 m: shout is heard, voice is not, whisper is not;
  - `a_distant_listener_loses_the_pitch` — at half the voice range the clarity is `32_768` (< `49_152`) and the pitch steps are flattened toward 0 but the energy survives;
  - `a_far_listener_loses_the_words` — below `word_drop_q16` the words are dropped and the listener hears an empty line (not a zero-energy line);
  - `a_murky_listener_hears_the_words_but_not_the_act` — below `act_clarity_q16` the copy's `act` is `None` while `intent` may still be `Some`;
  - `radio_dropout_is_deterministic` — the same utterance and the same listener drop the same words on every run and on every machine (the hash, not an RNG);
  - `enemies_overhear_voice` — an enemy within the voice range gets a degraded copy of a voice utterance, and its squad's knowledge records the told fact;
  - `heard_appears_in_the_score_view` — an utterance that ended inside the view window is in `view.heard`, and one that ended before it is not.

### Task 4: Utterances from agents

**Interfaces:**
- `lm-agents/src/utter.rs` (new):
  - `pub struct UtterPlan { pub act: SpeechAct, pub addressee: Addressee, pub intent: Intent, pub affect: Affect, pub subject: Subject, pub should_speak: bool }` — what the decision model chose.
  - `pub fn plan_from_gates(answers: &Answers, menu: &Menu, ctx: &GateCtx) -> Result<UtterPlan, Refusal>` — the **gates** (D37) do the choosing, in the same System One call as the action gates:
    - `gesture` (Choice) → `SpeechAct`: `Command` | `Question` | `Statement` | `Callout`;
    - `attention` (Choice) → `Addressee`: `Squad` | `All` | `Agent(<id>)`;
    - `intention` (Score, idle→urgent) → `Affect`: `Urgent` above `65_536`, `Uncertain` in the middle, `Calm` below — **bucketed by code**, never by the model;
    - `intent` (Choice) → `Intent`: `Order` | `Ack` | `Warn` | `Query` | `Report`;
    - `subject` (Choice) → the `Subject`, built from the **menu** (the beacon options) — the model picks an option that exists, never a free target;
    - every one of these carries a **presence gate (Noul)** (D53): "is this even here?" — a Noul of ~0 makes `should_speak` false.
  - `pub trait Realizer { fn realize(&self, plan: &UtterPlan, facts: &Facts) -> Result<String, Refusal>; }` — the wording. Two implementations:
    - `TemplateRealizer` (the **default**) — a phrasebook table keyed by `(act, intent, subject kind)`, rendered into OPAL text; a missing entry is a refusal, not a fallback phrase;
    - `LlmRealizer` — **LFM2-1.2B on the Thor** (`:8092`), asked for OPAL text only; the output is parsed by `opal::parse`, and **any parse failure is a refusal** (D26) — the bad line never reaches the log;
    - `AutoRealizer` — the template first; the LLM only if the phrasebook has no entry for the `(act, intent)` pair; silence if the LLM fails.
  - `pub fn pick_realizer(cfg: &AgentsConfig) -> Box<dyn Realizer>` — `utter.realizer = template | llm | auto` (default `auto`).
- **The phrasebook** (`assets/harness/default/phrasebook.toml`, loaded from the harness like `gates.toml`): entries keyed by `"{act}.{intent}"`, each a list of templates with `{subject}` and `{direction}` placeholders. It is **doctrine**: phase 5's reviewer may edit it (D25/D47), and the harness commit is pinned in the header (D27).
- **The airtime budget** (`TalkParams::airtime_s`, default 20 s per agent per round):
  - the sim adds each applied utterance's `duration_ticks` to the speaker's airtime;
  - an utterance that would take the speaker **over** the budget is **refused** by the sim (counted in `SimStats::talk_refusals`) — the speaker says nothing, and the sim keeps playing (the reflex continues);
  - `airtime_on_exhaust = "reflex"` → the agent keeps acting on reflexes; `"freeze"` → the agent stops acting;
  - the Thor's `Mind` checks the budget **before** submitting, so a refusal is rare, and the sim's backstop is the authority (both sides agree because the budget is in the header);
  - the per-agent counter lives in `Agent` (`airtime_ticks: u32`) and is **covered by the state hash** — it changes who may speak, so it is game state, not bookkeeping (the talk *records* of Task 8 are bookkeeping and are not hashed).
- **Pre-drafting (D31)**: when a `Heard` utterance is `ongoing` and names this agent as the addressee, the `Mind` may enqueue its reply's think request **at once**, with `apply_tick = heard.ended_tick + 1` (just after the last word) — so the reply is already on the runway instead of a full think latency later. The reply's content still goes through the gates.
- **Silence as an option**: when the presence gate (or the airtime budget, or a refusal) says no, **no utterance decision is emitted** — nothing is logged, nothing is spent.

  - `the_gates_pick_the_speech_act` — a scripted `Answers` gives `Command` for `gesture`, `Squad` for `attention`, `Urgent` for a high `intention` score and `Order` for `intent`;
  - `the_presence_gate_silences_the_agent` — a Noul of ~0 gives `should_speak == false` and **no** utterance decision;
  - `the_subject_comes_from_the_menu` — an answer naming an option that is not on the menu is refused (`Refusal::MenuChanged`), never mapped to a neighbour;

### Task 6: Human voice in

**Interfaces:**
- The host's microphone path: **push-to-talk → capture → ASR → prosody → an `Utterance` → a `Decision`**:
  - `pub fn build_human_utterance(transcript: &Transcript, prosody: Option<&MeasuredProsody>, speaker: u16, p: &TalkParams) -> Utterance` — the words come from the ASR with their timings (scaled to ticks); the lanes come from the measured tracks when there are any, and the score is marked `Prosody::Measured`; with no prosody the score is marked `Prosody::Absent` (**not** zero lanes) and the words are still timed;
  - the utterance is submitted as `Decision { source: Source::Human(agent), kind: Kind::Utterance(u), provenance: Provenance::Human }` — so it is logged, hashed and replayed like any other decision.
- **Prosody capture: a service, not a port.** `communicator` is reused **as a local HTTP sidecar on the ProArt** (`POST /score`, WAV in → the measured tracks out), reached through `ProsodyPort`:
  - *Why a service:* `communicator` is Python with a heavy dependency set (`typesafe_sdk`, numpy, silero ONNX), it lives outside this Rust workspace, and its science is the measured tracks themselves. A Rust port would freeze one snapshot of that science into a second implementation and drift from it — exactly what D53 says not to do.
  - *Why local (the ProArt) and not the Thor:* the mic audio is 16 kHz mono and never leaves the machine; the judged tracks can be made locally with **tev1** (`local_tev.py`, fleet/08), so no cloud key and no LAN hop; the measured DSP is the same wherever it runs.
  - *The fallback:* if the service is absent or errors, the utterance is built **without** prosody (`Prosody::Absent`) and the HUD says so. The round never waits for it.
  - `MeasuredProsody { pitch_hz: Vec<(u32, f32-equivalent)>, energy: Vec<u32>, tempo_bpm: u16, pauses: Vec<(u32, u32)>, act: Option<SpeechAct>, affect: Affect }` — the host converts it to the lanes in fixed point before it reaches `lm-core` (the score itself is integer-only).
- **Mic health:** `pub fn mic_health(pcm: &[i16]) -> MicHealth` with `zero_fraction = count(sample == 0) / len`; `zero_fraction == 1.0` is a **perfect detector** for a dead mic (fleet/08), so `zero_fraction >= mic.zero_fraction_max` (default `0.99`) returns `VoiceError::MicDead`: the capture is refused, the HUD says "mic dead", and **nothing** is submitted (a dead mic would otherwise become a silent, misread command).
- **Turn end ≠ voice stop** (fleet/08, communicator's `turn.py`):
  - `voice_stop_ms = 150` — a pause (it divides the tempo; it does not end the turn);
  - `turn_end_ms = 3_000` — the turn ends after three seconds of sustained silence (communicator's `TURN_END_S`; six of its seven recorded corpora produce **no** boundary at 3 s, and the longest inter-utterance gap anywhere is 2.1 s);
  - a **one-second pause never ends the turn** (33.8 % of Casey's pauses are ≥ 1 s): `one_second_pause_does_not_end_the_turn`;
  - push-to-talk is the default (`human.ptt = true`): the turn ends when the key is released, and the key is cut at `audio.max_talk_ms = 6_000`;
  - a **command verb ends the turn immediately** — "a system that can only see silence will misread every human decision as a long pause" (communicator's `turn.py`).
- **Negations are refused, not interpreted** (D29): the command parser refuses a sentence containing a negation token (`not`, `n't`, `never`, `no`, `dont`, `don't`, `without`, `except`, `avoid`, `quit`) with a named refusal; "stop" alone is a verb (hold), "don't stop" is a refusal.
- **The tutor ladder** (D29, `human.tutor = off | ladder`, default `ladder`): `pub struct TutorLadder { tiers: Vec<Vec<String>>, uses_to_unlock: u32 }` with the shipped tiers — 1: `hold`, `wait`, `stay`; 2: `north`, `south`, `east`, `west`; 3: `follow`, `leader`; 4: `retreat`, `fall back`, `beacon` — a verb of tier *n* unlocks tier *n+1* after `uses_to_unlock` (default 3) uses. A locked verb is **refused with the tier it needs and how to unlock it**, never silently reinterpreted.
- [ ] Tests:
  - `push_to_talk_ends_the_turn_on_release` — the turn ends on the key release, not on a pause;
  - `one_second_pause_does_not_end_the_turn` — 1 000 ms of silence inside a turn keeps the turn open; `three_seconds_of_silence_ends_the_turn` closes it;
  - `a_command_verb_ends_the_turn_immediately` — "hold" closes the turn at once;
  - `a_dead_mic_is_refused` — an all-zero capture gives `VoiceError::MicDead` and submits nothing;
  - `a_live_mic_is_accepted` — a capture with no zeros passes the health check;
  - `the_human_utterance_is_logged_and_replays` — the human's utterance is in the log with `Provenance::Human` and replays to the same hashes;
  - `a_negation_is_refused` — "don't stop" is refused; the round's hash is unchanged by the refusal;

  - `no_prosody_is_absent_not_zero` — with no prosody service the score is `Prosody::Absent` and the words still carry their timings;

**Interfaces:**
- `lm-core/src/talk.rs` (new):
  - `pub struct TalkRecord { pub id: u64, pub speaker: u16, pub at_tick: Tick, pub ended_tick: Tick, pub channel: Channel, pub act: SpeechAct, pub intent: Intent, pub affect: Affect, pub subject: Subject, pub words: u16, pub duration_ticks: u32, pub listeners: Vec<ListenerRecord> }`, `ListenerRecord { pub listener: u16, pub clarity_q16: u32, pub words_heard: u16, pub act_known: bool, pub intent_known: bool, pub took_up: bool }`

- The definitions (all code-computed, D49 — no model, no guess):
  - **order uptake** — an utterance with `Intent::Order` and `Subject::Beacon(b)`: the addressee **takes it up** when, within `order_uptake_window_ticks` (150) of the utterance's end, the addressee's motor command becomes `GoTo` that beacon's cell. The uptake is observed by the sim, never inferred from the words;
  - **words per successful order** — the mean word count of the utterances that were taken up (0 when none);
  - **misread rate** — the share of `(utterance, listener)` pairs whose copy lost a word or an act/intent tag (`words_heard < words`, or `!act_known`, or `!intent_known`);
  - **airtime** — the ticks of speech per agent and per squad (the trailing rests do not count);
  - **uptake by speaker** — per speaker, the orders and the taken-up ones (who is worth listening to).
- The sim collects the records as it plays (`Sim::talk_records()`); the records are **not** hashed (like `SimStats`), so they do not change the state hash; the *knowledge* they change **is** hashed.
- `lm-learn` (phase 5) calls the same pure function over the records of a replayed log — one implementation, two callers.
